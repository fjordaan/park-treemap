# What's new in Park Tree Map

A plain-English look back at how the map has grown since it launched in May
2026, for anyone curious what's changed.

## November 2026

- **No more 4-photo cap.** Add as many photos to a tree as you like – the
  popup shows the first four with a "+N" badge, and the lightbox lets you
  browse (and, if you're an enrolled contributor, reorder or delete) every
  photo.
- **Shared tree links keep working after a tree is repositioned.**
  Previously, moving a tree's pin could quietly break a link someone had
  bookmarked or shared. It now finds the tree at its new spot instead.
- A few small polish fixes: tidier buttons in the photo viewer, and a
  couple of visual glitches ironed out.

## September – October 2026

- **Added a History panel** – a running log of every tree added, edited,
  moved, or photographed, open from the Info panel. Handy for seeing what's
  changed and when.
- Reliability improvements: photo loads now retry automatically if the
  first attempt fails, and an enrolment link is checked straight away
  instead of only failing later when you try to use it.

## August 2026

- **Nearby parks discovery.** Zoom out far enough and you'll see other Park
  Tree Map parks nearby, with a tap-through link. Bayonne Park, Lillie Road
  Recreation Ground, Fulham Cemetery, and Normand Park have all joined.
- **Added free-text search**, so you can jump straight to a specific tree
  by name or number.
- **Photo dates.** Uploaded photos keep their original camera date, shown
  in the lightbox.
- **Swipe to browse photos** on touchscreens.
- **"Refine tree count"** – exclude certain trees (e.g. ones that have
  died) from the headline count without hiding them from the map.
- The project's brochure/about page moved to its own site,
  [parktreemap.org](https://parktreemap.org/).

## June – July 2026

- **Reposition a tree** by dragging its pin to the correct spot, if it was
  placed slightly wrong.
- Scientific-name autocomplete suggests the matching common name while
  adding a tree.
- **Zoom and pan photos** in the lightbox with a tap and drag.
- **Edit and delete trees** directly from the map, for enrolled
  contributors.
- A loading spinner now shows during saves, so it's clear something's
  happening.

## May 2026 – Launch

- Park Tree Map launches: an interactive map of a park's trees, with
  species, tags, notes, and photos.
- **Trusted contributors** – enrol specific devices (yours, your
  volunteers') to add trees and photos directly from the map, without
  opening it up to anyone passing by.
- Photos can be added to a tree after it's first logged, not just when
  it's created.
- **Seasonal interest** – tag which months a tree looks its best, and
  filter the map by season.
- A one-tap share link, and an About panel with the park's description and
  contact details.
